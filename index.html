About us

About Everwood Supply

Everwood Supply is a Nashville-based supplier of exclusive, large-scale wood slabs, specializing in rare burl and live-edge pieces. We focus on sourcing distinctive materials with exceptional character, scale, and natural beauty - slabs designed to become the centerpiece of a space.

Our inventory is curated for designers, builders, and craftsmen seeking unique, hard-to-find wood for custom furniture and architectural applications. We prioritize quality, transparency, and long-term relationships, offering hands-on guidance to help match each slab to the right project.

Based in Nashville, we welcome in-person viewings by appointment and work with clients nationwide.
